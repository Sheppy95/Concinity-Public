# Concinnity Interactive

**Infrastructure for a Decentralized Future.**

Concinnity Interactive is the parent entity dedicated to building autonomous, verifiable, and transparent compute environments. Our mission is to strip away the bloat of modern high-level abstraction and return computing to a foundation of verifiable structural logic.

## The Ecosystem

Our ecosystem is divided into two primary operational domains:

### 1. Open Software Fleet (Public)

Tools designed to empower communities with localized, autonomous capabilities.

* **Archon Scribe:** A lightweight, high-performance TTS/STT engine.
* **SmithWorks:** A proprietary AI interface designed for direct interaction with specialized reasoning models.
* **LOGOs (Discord Bot):** An autonomous moderation and TTS/STT integration bot for community environments.

### 2. Freedom of Information (Public)

Infrastructure for transparency and verifiable data integrity.

* **LcF (Audit Tooling):** A core auditing framework providing transparency into black-box systems.
* **Web Browser Claims Extension:** A verification tool for confirming the provenance and integrity of web content.
* **Library of Alexandria:** An immutable, decentralized knowledge repository designed to preserve fundamental human information.

## Research & Development (Private)

*The following pillars are the proprietary core of Concinnity Interactive and are currently maintained in private repositories:*

* **HORIZON Architecture:** Our shift toward chipset-based operations, including **CinnityOS** and the **Corvis** foundational language.
* **AI Reasoning Core:** R&D into our proprietary **SSA (Semantic/Structural Reasoning Architecture)** and **SSE (Synaptic/Systemic Execution Engine)**.
* **Advanced Hardware:** Research into non-waterfall computing, specifically **Fractal RAM** and **Proof Optical Processing**.
